## Research Projects

### Tunisian Sign Language Gesture Recognition
**P2M Project – Sup'Com** | 2025 – 2026
*Computer Vision, Deep Learning & Edge AI*

**Research Objectives**:
Design and experimentation of a Computer Vision system for recognizing 26 words from Tunisian Sign Language, with deployment objective on mobile application.

**Key Contributions**:
- **Dataset Creation**: Built proprietary corpus of 3,338 images across 26 classes, collected from 3 participants
- **Visual Processing Pipeline**: Implemented hand detection, region of interest extraction, background removal, preprocessing, and metadata generation
- **Data Augmentation Study**: Experimental investigation of rotation, zoom, brightness, and contrast augmentation impact on model generalization
- **Cascade Architecture**: Designed vision architecture combining YOLOv8m for detection and YOLOv8m-cls for gesture classification
- **Performance Evaluation**: Achieved 98.29% Top-1 accuracy, 99.70% Top-5 accuracy, and ~18.6ms latency
- **Mobile Optimization**: Optimized and integrated model for real-time inference on mobile using TensorFlow Lite and Flutter

**Technical Stack**: Python, YOLOv8, TensorFlow Lite, Flutter, OpenCV

### V2V Channel Experimental Modeling
**Research Project – Sup'Com** | 2025 – 2026
*Supervisor: Pr. Nèji Youssef*

**Research Objectives**:
Statistical study of multipath propagation in urban and highway V2V scenarios.

**Key Contributions**:
- **Statistical Analysis**: Estimated characteristic parameters including Doppler, coherence time, and sampling frequency
- **Channel Modeling**: Analyzed and compared Rayleigh and Rice statistical models for channel characterization
- **Numerical Optimization**: Implemented distribution fitting using least squares and numerical optimization with Python and scipy
- **Channel Simulation**: Simulated multipath channels and experimentally evaluated BPSK transmission over AWGN, Rayleigh, and Rice channels

**Technical Stack**: Python, SciPy, NumPy, Statistical Analysis
